Golden Bay is defined by its coastline. Whether you want a safe swim with the kids or a windswept walk with seals for company, there's a beach for every mood.

## Pohara & Ligar Bay

Easy access, cafés nearby, and some of our most popular holiday homes. Perfect for sunset barbecues.

## Tata Beach & Wainui

Sheltered water and a gateway feel toward Abel Tasman. Pair a swim with the Wainui Falls walk.

## Patons Rock & Parapara

Quieter stretches with big skies — ideal if you want space without the crowds.

## Wharariki & the far north

Pack a picnic and leave time. The arches and seal colonies are worth every dusty kilometre.
