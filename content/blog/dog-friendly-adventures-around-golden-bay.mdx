Travelling with a dog shouldn't mean compromising on Golden Bay's best bits. Many of our homes welcome pets — look for the pets badge on the catalogue.

## Beach etiquette

Check local bylaws for seasonal dog rules. Early mornings and quieter beaches like stretches near Parapara are often the calmest.

## Walks that work on-lead

Coastal tracks and estuary edges give dogs plenty to sniff while you take in the scenery. Always carry water — summers here are drier than they look.
