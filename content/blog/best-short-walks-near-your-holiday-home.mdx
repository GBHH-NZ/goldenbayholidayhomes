You don't need a multi-day tramp to feel into Golden Bay's landscape. These short walks sit close to many of our homes.

## Te Waikoropupu Springs

Boardwalks, clear water, and an easy grade — suitable for most fitness levels.

## Wainui Falls

A little more effort, a big payoff. Wear shoes with grip after rain.

## Coastal ambling

From your bach door: Pohara to Ligar, or Tata's shoreline at low tide. No DOC booking required — just sunscreen and curiosity.
