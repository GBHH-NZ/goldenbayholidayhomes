Golden Bay weather can turn quickly. Here's how we spend the grey days.

## Go underground

Rāwhiti Cave and other limestone spots are atmospheric when the sky is heavy.

## Town time

Tākaka's cafés, galleries, and the museum make an easy half-day. Collingwood is a good northern base before Farewell Spit tours.

## Bach rhythm

Hotel linen, a heat pump, and a board game — sometimes the best day is staying in. That's what holiday homes are for.
