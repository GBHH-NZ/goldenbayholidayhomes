Evenings in Golden Bay: simple, beautiful, unforgettable.

Kia ora and welcome! I’m Katja, and together with my family we run Golden Bay Holiday Homes here in beautiful Golden Bay, New Zealand. We created this blog to share not only our homes, but also the magic of this place — the beaches, bush walks, local food, and hidden gems that make Golden Bay so special. If you’d like to get to know us better, you can read more about our story on our [About Us](/about-us/) page.

Golden Bay is a corner of Aotearoa where life moves at a slower pace. From the crystal-clear waters of Te Waikoropupū Springs to the wild beauty of Wharariki Beach, there are endless places to explore. But it’s not only about the landscapes — it’s also about the people, the art, and the relaxed vibe that keeps visitors coming back. If you’d like even more inspiration, check out our [Explore Golden Bay](/explore-golden-bay/) page.

Through this blog, we’ll share travel tips, favourite cafés and restaurants, family-friendly adventures, and behind-the-scenes stories from running holiday homes in this unique part of New Zealand. Whether you’re planning your first trip, or returning for another stay, we hope you’ll find inspiration here.

Thanks for joining us — and welcome to Golden Bay!

— Katja, Golden Bay Holiday Homes
